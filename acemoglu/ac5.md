# Acemoglu風イントロの構成要素④：Key Results

## 🔹 このセクションの役割
**Key Resultsセクション**では、論文の主な貢献（特に理論モデルや実証分析による新規発見）を要点を押さえて読者に提示する。  
📌 読み手が**「この論文の価値」**を一目で把握できるようにするのが目的。

---

## 📊 1. パラグラフ数と構成

- **1〜2段落**が標準。

### 🔸 1段落構成（理論中心型）：
理論モデルから導出された主要な結果をコンパクトに提示。

### 🔸 2段落構成（理論＋実証 or カルブレーション型）：

- **第1段落**：モデルの設計と主結果のサマリー  
- **第2段落**：数値結果や実証結果、もしくはモデルの応用の簡単な説明

---

## 📏 2. 各パラグラフの文章量（行数）

- 各段落 **6〜10行**が典型的。
- 数式や図表の説明が入るとやや短めに。

---

## 🗣 3. よく使われる語彙・構文

### 🔸 結果提示の典型句
- “In this paper, we show that…”
- “Our main result is that…”
- “We develop a model that…”
- “We document that…”
- “The model generates dynamics consistent with…”
- “The analysis yields several novel implications…”

### 🔸 実証 or 定量分析のリンク
- “Our calibration shows that…”
- “Quantitatively, we find that…”
- “We simulate the model to…”
- “The empirical results confirm that…”

---

## 🧭 4. 段落ごとの構成要素（典型パターン）

| 段落 | 機能 | 内容例 |
|------|------|--------|
| 第1段落 | モデルの概要と理論的主張 | モデルの仕掛け、比較静学の直観、理論的インプリケーションの要約 |
| 第2段落 | 数値例・定量的含意 | カリブレーション・実証との整合性・モデルの応用例などの報告 |

---

## ✏️ 5. Key Resultsテンプレ（2段落型）

### ✅ Key Resultsテンプレート

[Paragraph 1]  
We develop a model in which [core mechanism], and show that this framework delivers [main theoretical result]. In particular, the model implies that [novel implication 1] and [novel implication 2]. These results emerge as a consequence of [core assumptions or frictions].

[Paragraph 2]  
We calibrate the model using [data source / parameter values] and find that the equilibrium dynamics are consistent with [empirical patterns or stylized facts]. In particular, the model matches [fact 1] and predicts [fact 2]. These quantitative results underscore the relevance of our mechanism in explaining [broader theme or puzzle].

## 🔄 6. 他セクションとの接続文例
Key Resultsの最後では、以下のような文で「Literature Review」や「Model」のセクションへとスムーズに橋渡し：

- “These findings relate to a large literature on [topic], which we discuss in the next section.”

- “Before proceeding to the model, we review the related literature on [subject].”

## ✅ チェックリスト（Key Results執筆時）
☑️ モデルの仕掛けとメカニズムが明確に説明されているか？

☑️ 結果が理論・実証の両側面から端的に示されているか？

☑️ セクションが簡潔にまとまっていて、読み手の注意を惹くようになっているか？

☑️ 読者が「この論文の価値」を理解できる要約になっているか？

📌 イントロ全体の中でも、読者の記憶に残る「勝負どころ」セクション。インパクトと明瞭さが重要。

## アセモグル風イントロの第4要素「Key Results」の穴埋め形式テンプレート（2段落構成・各10行）

### 🧩 段落1：モデルの仕掛けと理論的主張（10行）
We develop a dynamic model in which [core mechanism or distortion] plays a central role.  
The model features [main structural element, e.g., incomplete markets, borrowing constraints, long-term debt].  
Our key innovation is to allow for [specific friction or heterogeneity], which is often abstracted away in prior work.  
Within this framework, we show that [main result 1], even in the absence of [standard channel or assumption].  
This result emerges from the interaction between [mechanism A] and [mechanism B], generating [key theoretical outcome].  
In particular, the model implies that [novel implication 1], which contrasts with [baseline prediction].  
It also generates [novel implication 2], which helps rationalize [empirical puzzle or stylized fact].  
These dynamics highlight the importance of [economic insight] in shaping [outcome variable].  
Our theoretical contribution is to formalize how [core feature] leads to [unexpected result].  
This sets the stage for a quantitative exploration of the model’s predictions.

### ❓ 段落2：カリブレーション／定量分析と含意（10行）
To assess the quantitative relevance of our mechanism, we calibrate the model to [country/sample] data.  
Key parameters are disciplined using [data source or empirical estimates], and the model is simulated under [conditions].  
We find that the calibrated economy replicates key patterns in the data, including [stylized fact A] and [fact B].  
In particular, the model closely matches the evolution of [variable] in response to [shock or reform].  
It also generates plausible magnitudes for [outcome of interest], consistent with existing estimates.  
These results underscore the explanatory power of [mechanism] in accounting for [economic puzzle or trend].  
We further show that removing [friction or channel] significantly attenuates the model’s fit to the data.  
This suggests that [core insight] is not only theoretically relevant, but quantitatively significant.  
The framework also lends itself to counterfactual analysis, which we exploit in later sections.  
Taken together, these findings establish a robust link between [mechanism] and [observed phenomenon]. These findings relate to a large literature on [topic], which we discuss in the next.
