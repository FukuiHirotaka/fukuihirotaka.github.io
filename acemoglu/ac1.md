# アセモグル風イントロの作り方

## 概要

このガイドは、Daron Acemogluの論文スタイルに倣って、説得力と明快さのあるイントロダクションを書くための構成・文体・語彙・テンプレートを体系的にまとめたものです。

---

## 1. 基本構成（6パート）

Acemogluが多くの論文で用いる典型的な構成：

1. **Big Picture（現象の提起）**
2. **Key Attribute（特徴的な観察事実）**
3. **Motivation（なぜ重要か／何が分かっていないか）**
4. **Key Results（本論文の主な発見）**
5. **Literature Review（位置づけ）**
6. **Roadmap（構成案内）**

---

## 2. スタイルの特徴

- **明快でダイレクトな表現**（比喩・婉曲表現は少ない）
- **短いパラグラフで論点を明示**
- **実証・理論に関わらず、仮説検証型の語り口**
- **政策的・社会的含意を明示するフレーズが多い**
- **1パラ目はできるだけ"hook"がある**

---

## 3. パート別テンプレートと例文

### ① Big Picture（導入）

> **Template:**  
> A central question in [FIELD] is why [STRIKING PHENOMENON].  
> [COUNTRY/REGION] provides a particularly revealing setting to investigate this issue.

**例:**  
A central question in international macroeconomics is why some developing countries experience recurrent debt crises even after implementing market-oriented reforms. Ghana provides a particularly revealing setting to investigate this issue.

---

### ② Key Attribute（特異な事実の提示）

> **Template:**  
> This paper begins by documenting [SALIENT FACT/FACTUAL PATTERN], which is surprising because [EXPLANATION].

**例:**  
This paper begins by documenting that Ghana’s public debt surged dramatically following the 2006 liberalization, even before the global shocks of the 2020s. This pattern is surprising because liberalization is typically associated with fiscal restraint and capital inflows.

---

### ③ Motivation（問題意識）

> **Template:**  
> This observation raises an important question: [CENTRAL QUESTION].  
> While previous research has studied [RELATED TOPIC], much less is known about [GAP].

**例:**  
This observation raises an important question: Did liberalization sow the seeds of future instability, or was Ghana simply unlucky? While previous research has studied the effects of external shocks on emerging market debt, much less is known about the long-term consequences of capital account liberalization in low-income, resource-dependent countries.

---

### ④ Key Results（本研究の貢献）

> **Template:**  
> This paper develops [MODEL TYPE], calibrated to [COUNTRY DATA], and evaluates [KEY COUNTERFACTUALS].  
> The analysis yields three main findings.  
> First, [...].  
> Second, [...].  
> Third, [...].

**例:**  
This paper develops a continuous-time sovereign default model, calibrated to Ghanaian macroeconomic data, and evaluates two key counterfactuals.  
The analysis yields three main findings. First, the 2006 liberalization substantially expanded Ghana’s borrowing capacity. Second, the resulting debt path was highly sensitive to external shocks. Third, absent the global rate hikes, default might have been avoided even under expanded borrowing capacity.

---

### ⑤ Literature Review（先行研究との関係）

> **Template:**  
> This paper is related to three strands of literature.  
> First, [...].  
> Second, [...].  
> Third, [...].  
> The most closely related paper is [CITATION], which [...].  
> This paper differs by [...].

**例:**  
This paper is related to three strands of literature. First, the literature on sovereign debt and default (Eaton and Gersovitz, 1981; Aguiar and Gopinath, 2006). Second, the literature on capital account liberalization and macroeconomic fragility (Kaminsky and Schmukler, 2008; Eichengreen, 2001). Third, recent quantitative studies of debt dynamics using continuous-time models (Bornstein, 2020).  
The most closely related paper is Aguiar et al. (2016), which provides a comprehensive review of sovereign debt crises. This paper differs by focusing on a single historical episode using a calibrated model to assess causal links.

---

### ⑥ Roadmap（構成）

> **Template:**  
> The remainder of the paper is organized as follows. Section 2 provides [...]. Section 3 presents [...]. Section 4 describes [...]. Section 5 discusses [...]. Section 6 concludes.

**例:**  
The remainder of the paper is organized as follows. Section 2 provides institutional background on Ghana’s liberalization and fiscal trajectory. Section 3 presents the model. Section 4 describes the calibration and counterfactual simulations. Section 5 discusses policy implications. Section 6 concludes.

## 4. よく使われる語彙・構文

- A central question in economics is...
- This paper argues that...
- A striking pattern is that...
- This finding is surprising because...
- This paper develops a model of...
- The analysis yields three key insights...
- The most closely related paper is...
- This paper differs by...

---

## 5. 実践Tips

- **構造をテンプレ化して、最初は「型」を意識して書く**
- **“問い”を明確に据える**ことで読者の関心を引く
- **Abstractとの整合性**を保ちつつ、イントロの方がストーリー重視
- **専門的すぎない語彙**で書き、査読者以外の読者にも読ませる意識を忘れずに
- **段落のはじめの一文で主張を言い切る**のが重要（Acemoglu流）

## 7. パラグラフ構成・長さのガイドライン（定量的テンプレ）

### イントロ全体のボリューム感（Acemoglu論文の傾向）

- **全体の長さ**：3〜5ページ（ダブルスペース、Times New Roman, 12pt換算）
- **段落数**：おおよそ6〜8段落
- **各段落の行数**：だいたい5〜10行（Wordで換算）

---

### 各パートの段落配分（標準構成）

| パート | 内容 | 段落数 | 目安行数 | コメント |
|-------|------|--------|----------|----------|
| ① Big Picture | 現象の導入、背景 | 1段落 | 6〜8行 | 強い"hook"があるとベスト |
| ② Key Attribute | 具体的な事実や異常なパターン | 1段落 | 5〜7行 | データやグラフを軽く紹介してもよい |
| ③ Motivation | 中心的な問いとギャップ提示 | 1〜2段落 | 8〜14行 | 最も論理的に力を入れる部分 |
| ④ Key Results | モデル・分析・主な発見 | 1〜2段落 | 8〜14行 | 結論めいたことも簡潔に書く |
| ⑤ Literature Review | 位置づけ、差別化 | 1〜2段落 | 8〜12行 | 関連文献をストランドごとに整理 |
| ⑥ Roadmap | 構成案内 | 1段落 | 4〜6行 | 省略されることもあるが基本は入れる |

---

### 補足アドバイス

- **1パラ目（Big Picture）は特に重要**：読者の関心を一気に引き込む“導火線”になる。
- **Key Resultsは「First, Second, Third」などで明快に**：箇条書きっぽく書いても論理が通る。
- **Literature Reviewでは“差別化ポイント”を必ず入れる**："This paper differs by..."など。
- **段落のつなぎには接続句を自然に**："This observation raises...", "In contrast,", "While previous work..."

---

### イントロ全体の構成イメージ（例）

```text
Paragraph 1: Big picture & hook
Paragraph 2: Key attribute (stylized fact)
Paragraph 3–4: Motivation and main question
Paragraph 5: Model overview and key results
Paragraph 6: Contribution to the literature
Paragraph 7: Roadmap of the paper
