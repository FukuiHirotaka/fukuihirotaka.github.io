# ✅ Acemoglu風イントロ：Motivationセクション 共通構造テンプレート分析

## 🔹 Motivationとは？
提示されたstylized factやKey Attributeが**なぜ理論的・政策的に重要なのか**、そして**既存文献ではなぜそれが十分に説明されていないのか**を明確にするパート。

🎯 「何がわかっていないのか？」という問いに答え、本論文の中心的な問いと貢献を導く。

---

## 📊 1. パラグラフ数と構成

- **1〜2段落**が主流。

### 🔸 1段落型：
問いと既存研究のギャップを一気に提示。

### 🔸 2段落型：

- **第1段落**：問いの提示と既存研究への短評  
- **第2段落**：ギャップの明確化と本研究の貢献の序章（「我々はこれに取り組む」的導入）

---

## 📏 2. 各パラグラフの行数

- **5〜9行程度**
- Key Attributeからの流れを受けて比較的短く、**問いの明確さと意義**が重視される。

---

## 🗣 3. よく使われる語彙・構文

### 🔸 問いの提示
- “This raises the question of whether...”
- “A central question is whether...”
- “Why does this happen?”
- “Yet we know relatively little about...”
- “The extent to which X causes Y remains unclear.”

### 🔸 ギャップ強調・リテラチャー批判
- “Despite a large literature on...”
- “There is surprisingly little work that...”
- “Existing models do not provide a satisfactory explanation for...”
- “This aspect has been largely overlooked in the literature.”

### 🔸 本研究への導入
- “We take a first step in addressing this issue by...”
- “In this paper, we develop a framework that...”
- “Our approach allows us to...”

---

## 🧭 4. パラグラフ構成のタイプ別分類

| 段落 | 機能 | 内容例 |
|------|------|--------|
| 第1段落 | 問いの明示と文献批判 | 「〜という事実を考えると、〜という問いが重要となる。しかしながら既存研究は〜に偏っており、〜が十分に解明されていない」 |
| 第2段落 | 本研究のターゲット定義 | 「この問題に取り組むために、我々は〜という枠組みを構築する。これにより〜という観点から分析できる」 |

---

## ✏️ 5. Motivationセクションの典型テンプレ（マクロ系向け）

### ✅ Motivationテンプレ（2段落型）

[Paragraph 1]  
This pattern raises the central question of whether [mechanism or causal link]. Despite a large literature on [topic X], relatively little is known about [specific channel or deviation from standard models]. Existing studies have mostly focused on [Y], but have not explored [Z], which appears to be crucial in explaining [observed pattern].

[Paragraph 2]  
We take a first step in addressing this issue by developing a model that incorporates [key extension]. This allows us to systematically study how [mechanism] operates under [conditions]. Our approach sheds new light on [broader implication or policy relevance].

## 🧩 6. 構成要素との連動性
Key Attribute → Motivation → Model の自然な接続が重要。

Motivationの語尾は次セクションへの橋渡し：
- “Our main findings can be summarized as follows.”

- “We formalize this idea in a model...”

- “To explore this, we construct a dynamic framework where...”

✅ チェックリスト（Motivation執筆時）
- ☑️ 「問い」が明確に提示されているか？

- ☑️ その問いはKey Attributeから自然に導かれているか？

- ☑️ ギャップや未解明な部分が明確に指摘されているか？

- ☑️ 読者に「この論文が必要だ」と感じさせる構成になっているか？

- 📌 このセクションは、**問い・ギャップ・貢献の「重力中心」**であり、イントロの中核をなす部分です。

## アセモグル風イントロの第3構成要素「Motivation」の穴埋めテンプレート（2段落構成・各9行）

### 🧩 段落1：問いの提示と既存文献のギャップ批判（9行）
This pattern raises the fundamental question of whether [core mechanism or causal link].  
Despite an extensive literature on [broad topic], our understanding of [specific phenomenon or deviation] remains limited.  
Existing studies primarily emphasize [mainstream theory or factor], often under the assumption that [standard condition].  
Yet, this approach struggles to account for [empirical anomaly or stylized fact].  
Several recent works attempt to bridge this gap, but they typically focus on [related but different mechanism].  
Moreover, little attention has been paid to [key dimension or institutional factor], which seems central in many contexts.  
This neglect is particularly puzzling given that [empirical or theoretical justification].  
As a result, we lack a coherent explanation for [the observed puzzle or regularity].  
This motivates a closer investigation into the mechanisms behind [specific dynamic or failure].

### ❓ 段落2：我々の貢献の予告と理論枠組みの導入（9行）
We address this issue by developing a framework that integrates [key feature or friction] into a standard [model class] setting.  
Our approach departs from the conventional view by allowing for [specific assumption relaxation or innovation].  
This enables us to systematically explore how [mechanism] shapes [economic outcome or behavior].  
In particular, we focus on [subchannel or comparative dimension], which has received little attention thus far.  
The model generates predictions consistent with [stylized fact or empirical pattern], offering a new lens on [debate].  
Moreover, it allows for a range of counterfactuals that shed light on [policy or institutional implication].  
By formalizing this logic, we aim to clarify the conditions under which [phenomenon] emerges and persists.  
Our framework provides a tractable yet flexible platform to investigate [broader topic].  
In doing so, it lays the groundwork for a richer understanding of [main contribution or open question].
