# Acemoglu風イントロの構成要素②：Key Attribute

## 🔹 Key Attributeとは？
読者に「なぜこの問いが重要か？」を納得させるための核心的特徴や観察事実（stylized facts, empirical puzzles, structural tensions）を提示するパート。Big Pictureが外観なら、Key Attributeは焦点である。

---

## 📊 1. パラグラフ数と構成

- **1〜2段落**が主流。

### 🔸 1段落型：
典型的事実を列挙して問題の本質を導出。

### 🔸 2段落型：
1段落目でstylized factを提示し、2段落目でその理論的含意または既存理論とのギャップを議論。

---

## 🧱 2. 各パラグラフの行数

- 一般的に**6〜10行程度**（論文2段組ベース）。
- 図表への言及（"Figure 1 shows..."）があるとやや長くなる傾向。

---

## 🗣 3. よく使われる語彙・構文

### 🔸 Stylized Fact提示に使われる表現
- “A striking feature of [phenomenon] is that…”
- “This pattern is particularly surprising because…”
- “We begin by documenting...”
- “We document a robust negative relationship between…”
- “This fact raises important questions about...”
- “This empirical regularity suggests that...”

### 🔸 強調ワード
- **striking**, **surprising**, **robust**, **persistent**, **puzzling**, **systematic**

### 🔸 データに基づいた観察への導入構文
- “Figure X illustrates…”
- “Table X reports…”
- “As shown in Figure/Table X…”

---

## 🧭 4. パラグラフ構成のタイプ別分類

| 段落 | 機能 | 内容例 |
|------|------|--------|
| 第1段落 | 主観察の提示 | スタイライズドファクトの提示、既存理論との食い違いの指摘 |
| 第2段落 | 解釈 or 理論的インプリケーション | なぜ重要なのか、なぜ説明が難しいのか、または現象の解釈例 |

---

## 🪜 5. Key Attributeのあとにくる構成要素との接続

- “This motivates our analysis…”
- “In order to understand this pattern, we develop a model...”
- “These facts point to a need for…”

➡️ **Key Attributeは理論・モデル構築の動機として配置される。**

---

## ✏️ 6. 典型的テンプレ（書き換え可能な雛形）

### ✅ Key Attributeテンプレ（1段落型）

A striking feature of [topic] is that [stylized fact]. This pattern is robust across [dimensions, time, countries, etc.], as we document in [Figure/Table]. Interestingly, this finding is hard to reconcile with [dominant theory or common belief], which would predict [opposite or no effect]. This mismatch suggests that [deeper mechanism or overlooked factor] might be at play.

### ✅ Key Attributeテンプレ（2段落型）

[Paragraph 1]
We begin by documenting a puzzling fact: [X]. This pattern is robust and appears across [contexts or datasets]. For example, [specific empirical fact].

[Paragraph 2]
This fact is difficult to explain within the standard framework of [literature Y], which typically emphasizes [Z]. One possible interpretation is that [hypothesized mechanism], motivating our theoretical investigation.

## アセモグル風イントロにおける第2構成要素「Key Attribute」の穴埋めテンプレート（2段落構成・各10行）

### 🧩 段落1：Empirical Pattern / Stylized Fact の提示（10行）
A striking feature of [phenomenon or variable] is that [stylized fact or empirical relationship].  
This pattern emerges consistently across [countries, time periods, industries], and is robust to [controls/definitions].  
For instance, data from [source] show that [empirical observation or trend].  
Figure 1 illustrates that [clear and specific description of empirical fact].  
This pattern holds even when accounting for [confounding factor or alternative explanation].  
Surprisingly, this is not limited to [context A], but extends to [context B] as well.  
Such consistency raises doubts about explanations based solely on [existing theory or mechanism].  
In contrast to conventional wisdom, the data suggest that [unexpected direction or anomaly].  
This persistent pattern calls for a deeper examination of [mechanism or structural tension].  
It is this regularity that serves as the empirical starting point for our analysis.

### ❓ 段落2：理論的含意・齟齬・解釈の導出（10行）
This empirical regularity is difficult to reconcile with the standard predictions of [theory or framework],  
which generally attributes [phenomenon] to [channel or condition].  
According to this logic, we should observe [expected pattern], not [what is actually observed].  
Several extensions have tried to address this, but they often assume [strong assumption or constraint].  
One possible explanation for the observed discrepancy lies in [alternative mechanism or overlooked factor].  
For example, [mechanism] may generate effects that mimic [stylized fact] without relying on [traditional channel].  
This interpretation aligns with recent evidence from [related study or literature].  
Nevertheless, a formal theoretical framework that incorporates this logic remains underdeveloped.  
To address this gap, we construct a model that embeds [key feature or mechanism].  
This framework allows us to assess whether [mechanism] can account for the patterns documented above.
