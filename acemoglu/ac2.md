# Acemoglu風イントロの構成要素①：「Big Picture」

## 📌 目的
イントロ冒頭の1〜2段落で、「この研究がどんな“現象”に立脚しているのか」「なぜそれが興味深い／重要なのか」を、歴史的・理論的・実証的背景を用いて提示する。

---

## 🧱 典型構造（段落数・機能）

| 段落番号 | 内容の役割                         | 構成要素                                   |
|----------|------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| 第1段落  | 現象の導入（big picture）          | 背景事実の提示 / stylized factの紹介        |
| 第2段落  | 現象の解釈とギャップ・問いの導出   | 既存研究の限界 / Puzzleの導出 / 誘導的問い  |

※ 論文によっては1段落に凝縮されるケースもあり（簡潔型）

---

## 📐 行数と長さの目安

- 各段落：**6〜12行**（2カラムPDF換算）
- 最初の文は**1行〜2行の簡潔な文**で始める
- 後半にかけて**構文が複雑になり、文の密度が増加する**

---

## 🗣 よく使われる語彙・構文

### ■ 文頭構文（hookの導入に使われる）

- "There is now a large literature on ..."
- "Over the past decades, ..."
- "Recent decades have witnessed ..."
- "A central question in [field] is ..."
- "Historically, ..."（歴史的背景から入るパターン）

### ■ stylized fact の提示

- "[Phenomenon] has increased significantly since ..."
- "Despite reforms, [problem] persists in many countries."
- "Existing trends suggest a puzzling pattern: ..."

### ■ 問いの誘導と導入

- "This fact raises an important question: ..."
- "This observation is at odds with ..."
- "Yet, it remains unclear why/how ..."
- "Why does this occur?" / "What explains this?"

---

## 🧰 テンプレート本文例（書き換え可能な雛形）

### 🧩 段落1：Big Pictureの導入


There is now a large and influential literature on [broad topic]. Over the past few decades, [phenomenon] has become increasingly prevalent, with [specific empirical trend or historical fact]. For example, [stylized fact or example].

This development has generated both enthusiasm and concern. While some view it as [positive interpretation], others argue that it has exacerbated [problem or tension].

### ❓ 段落2：問題提起・ギャップ・問いの誘導

This trend raises an important question: [core question of the paper]. While existing theories have made progress in explaining [related issue], they often overlook [missing dimension / key friction]. Moreover, little is known about [specific puzzle relevant to your paper].

Understanding this issue is particularly important because [why it matters: policy, theory, broader debates].

🧾 使用例（簡略版）

A central question in development economics is why some countries achieve sustained growth while others remain trapped in poverty. Despite similar initial conditions, trajectories have diverged dramatically over the past half century. For example, while country A has experienced rapid industrialization, country B has stagnated.

This pattern raises a puzzle: why do some economies capitalize on reform while others fail? Existing theories emphasize institutions and capital accumulation, but they struggle to explain the timing and persistence of these divergent paths. This paper addresses this gap by analyzing...

## アセモグル風イントロの「Big Picture」パートの穴埋めテンプレート（2段落・各12行）

### 🧩 段落1：現象の導入とStylized Factの提示（12行）

Over the past [number] years, there has been growing attention to [broad phenomenon or policy shift].  
This is particularly evident in [region/country context], where [notable trend or reform] has taken place.  
For example, [specific reform or event] marked a turning point in [economic or political outcome].  
Despite such efforts, [persistent issue] continues to shape the landscape in unexpected ways.  
Data from [source or time period] show that [stylized fact #1], while [contrast fact #2].  
This juxtaposition reveals a puzzling dynamic: [succinct statement of contradiction].  
Several countries have followed similar trajectories, yet the outcomes differ substantially.  
One notable instance is [country A vs. country B], where [divergent pattern].  
While some scholars argue that [optimistic interpretation], others warn of [concern].  
These contrasting perspectives underscore a central tension in [field of study].  
Why do seemingly similar policies yield such heterogeneous outcomes?  
This question forms the starting point of our inquiry.

### ❓ 段落2：問いの導出と既存研究の限界提示（12行）

Existing research has sought to explain these patterns through the lens of [dominant theory].  
This literature emphasizes [key mechanism or institutional factor], which indeed captures part of the story.  
However, it often neglects [friction, constraint, or empirical observation] that may be crucial in many contexts.  
In particular, little is known about how [specific mechanism or interaction] contributes to these outcomes.  
This omission is especially salient in [region/country], where [policy/economic feature] plays a central role.  
Moreover, the existing evidence tends to rely on [methodology or assumption], which limits its applicability.  
As a result, we lack a coherent framework to assess [specific outcome or puzzle].  
This paper seeks to fill that gap by focusing on [core research question].  
We ask: [precise, one-sentence research question].  
To answer this, we combine [data/method] with a model that incorporates [key feature].  
Our approach enables a systematic evaluation of [mechanism or counterfactual].  
Ultimately, we aim to shed new light on [broader implications or theoretical contribution].
