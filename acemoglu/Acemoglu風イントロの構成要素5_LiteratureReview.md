# ✅ Acemoglu風イントロ：Literature Reviewセクション 共通構造テンプレート分析

## 📌 1. このセクションの目的
この論文が属する分野（literature）を特定し、  
特に**近い先行研究との差異を明示**することで、  
自身の研究の**貢献（novelty）**と**正当性**を明確化する。

---

## 📊 2. パラグラフ数と構成要素

- **1～2パラグラフ構成**が一般的。

### 🔸 1パラグラフ構成：
主要な分野をざっと網羅しつつ、差異に焦点。

### 🔸 2パラグラフ構成：
- **第1段落**：広義の分野・学派を列挙  
- **第2段落**：直接的に関係の深い論文と本研究の違いを強調

---

## 📏 3. 各パラグラフの文章量

- **7～11行**が標準。  
- 先行研究の系譜が長い場合は少し長めになることも。

---

## 📚 4. 引用のスタイルと数

- **5～12本程度**を引用。  
- 短文で文献名のみを列挙する手法が多い。

### 🔸 引用スタイルの定型句
- “Our paper is related to…”
- “This work contributes to the literature on…”
- “This paper differs from…”
- “Most closely related is…”
- “This study builds on…”

---

## 🧠 5. よく使われる語彙・構文

| 用途 | 表現例 |
|------|--------|
| 広義の分野紹介 | “related to a large literature on…” / “a growing body of work…” |
| 近接研究との関係 | “builds on”, “extends”, “follows”, “is inspired by” |
| 差異の強調 | “differs from”, “departs from”, “introduces a novel mechanism…” |
| 独自性の提示 | “to the best of our knowledge”, “has not been studied in the context of…” |

---

## 🧭 6. 段落ごとの構成と機能

| 段落 | 内容の要約 |
|------|-------------|
| 第1段落 | 対象となる複数の文献領域の紹介（例：技術変化、国際貿易、スキルプレミアム） |
| 第2段落 | 特に関連が深い文献との比較・差異化、本研究のユニークさの強調 |

---

## ✏️ 7. Literature Review テンプレ（2段落型）

[Paragraph 1]  
Our paper is related to a broad literature on [field 1] (e.g., Author1, Year; Author2, Year), [field 2] (e.g., Author3, Year; Author4, Year), and [field 3] (e.g., Author5, Year; Author6, Year). This body of work has focused on [general mechanism or pattern], typically emphasizing [canonical assumptions or channels].

[Paragraph 2]  
More closely related to our study is the work by [Author A, Year], [Author B, Year], and [Author C, Year], who investigate [more specific mechanism]. While our paper shares the focus on [overlapping theme], we depart from this literature in two key respects. First, we [novelty #1]. Second, we [novelty #2]. To the best of our knowledge, no previous paper has examined [your specific contribution] in the context of [setting].

## 🧩 8. 先行研究との差異の提示方法（差別化パターン）

| パターン | 例文 |
|------|-------------|
|モデル構造の差 | “While previous models assume X, we allow for Y…” |
|分析対象の違い	| “Whereas earlier work focused on developed economies, we examine…” |
|経路・メカニズムの違い	| “We introduce a different mechanism linking A to B…” |
|エンピリカルな違い	| “Unlike prior studies that used cross-country data, we exploit firm-level variation…” |

## ✅ チェックリスト（Literature Review執筆時）
☑️ 関連する複数の分野に触れているか？

☑️ 自身の研究がそのどこに位置するかを示しているか？

☑️ 直接的な先行研究との「違い」と「貢献」が明確になっているか？

☑️ 引用文献はバランスよくカバーされているか？

📌 本セクションは、「自分の立ち位置」を宣言する場所。
説得力のある構成で読者の納得感を獲得することが鍵。

## アセモグル風イントロの第5要素「Literature Review」の穴埋め形式テンプレート（2段落構成・各11行）

### 🧩 段落1：広義の文献領域とその枠組み（11行）
Our paper contributes to several strands of the literature.  
First, it relates to a large body of work on [field 1], which has examined [canonical mechanism or trend] (e.g., Author1, Year; Author2, Year).  
Second, it connects to studies on [field 2], particularly those exploring [channel or structure] (e.g., Author3, Year; Author4, Year).  
A third related area is the literature on [field 3], which has emphasized [long-standing question or assumption] (e.g., Author5, Year; Author6, Year).  
This broad literature has highlighted the importance of [factor or mechanism] in shaping [economic outcome].  
Most of these studies rely on models assuming [assumption A], often abstracting from [important real-world feature].  
While they provide valuable insights, they tend to focus on [narrow scope or homogeneous setting].  
Recent contributions have started relaxing these assumptions, incorporating [dimension or friction] into the analysis.  
Yet, much of this work remains concentrated on [region/economy/context], leaving other settings underexplored.  
Moreover, few papers examine the interaction between [factor A] and [factor B], despite its theoretical relevance.  
Our study builds on these foundations, while extending the scope to [your novel context or combination].

### ❓ 段落2：近接研究との比較と自らの貢献の強調（11行）
More closely related to our approach are the studies by [Author A, Year], [Author B, Year], and [Author C, Year],  
who analyze [specific mechanism or empirical pattern] using [approach or model type].  
These papers provide important insights into [theme], often under the assumption that [constraint or structure].  
Our paper shares their focus on [overlapping topic], but differs in two key respects.  
First, we allow for [novelty 1], which is absent in their frameworks.  
Second, we apply our model to [context or data], which has received limited attention despite its relevance.  
This extension is crucial because [reason why it matters].  
Moreover, our framework permits a richer set of counterfactuals that illuminate [policy issue or dynamic].  
To the best of our knowledge, no prior work has examined [your contribution] in the context of [specific setting].  
In doing so, we provide new insights into [broader research question or policy relevance].  
Our analysis thus complements and extends the existing literature in meaningful ways.
